About tracking

When you use the class link, your completed checks, whether each code drill check passed, confidence ratings, and flashcard difficulty ratings are recorded automatically. Your instructor sees anonymous class totals to help decide what to revisit in class. Your name, your code, typed answers, and individual browsing history are not included in the report. Use the same browser to keep your progress and anonymous counts consistent.
